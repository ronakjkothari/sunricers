How this was built

Demand comes from spend-patterns-rice: for each shop, monthly card customers (RAW_NUM_CUSTOMERS) and daily spend (SPEND_BY_DAY), 2019-12 to 2024-12, 20,569 energy / food / water / venue businesses within 75 km of the 11 host-city centres. Heat comes from urban-heat-index-rice, averaged into 0.01° squares. Fixtures and stadiums come from the published 2026 schedule.

Sample data were transformed by the organisers — multiplicative noise on magnitudes, spatial jitter on coordinates. Footprints are estimated by multiplying customer counts by the hand-set factors in intensity_factors.csv (food 2.8 kWh, 25 L, 3.5 kg CO₂e per customer; energy 45 / 1.5 / 12; lodging 28 / 300 / 2; venue 4 / 15 / 2.5). This demonstrates method, not real conditions in any city.

The match-day effect (+30% within 2 km) was measured as game week ÷ normal week on the same shops across 566 NFL home games at these stadiums (+33%) and 19 Copa América 2024 matches (+27%). A citywide visitor lift could not be separated from noise (Copa 2024 host cities vs Boston / Philadelphia / Seattle, ±8%). Nothing on this page is a forecast; a 2026 match is shown against the same month of 2024.

Visitor share uses CUSTOMER_HOME_CITY: the share of a shop's card customers whose home city is outside the metro's states, 2022–2024, shops with 20+ such customers. Readiness scores use the team's Plan D scoring on per-shop rates.
