How this was built

Demand comes from spend-patterns-rice: for each shop, monthly card customers (RAW_NUM_CUSTOMERS) and daily spend (SPEND_BY_DAY), December 2019 to December 2024, covering 20,569 energy, food, water, and venue businesses within 75 km of the 11 host-city centres. Heat comes from urban-heat-index-rice, averaged into 0.01° squares. Fixtures and stadiums come from the published 2026 schedule.

Footprints multiply customer counts by the factors in intensity_factors.csv (food 2.8 kWh, 25 L, 3.5 kg CO₂e per customer; energy 45 / 1.5 / 12; lodging 28 / 300 / 2; venue 4 / 15 / 2.5).

The match-day effect (+30% within 2 km) was measured as game week divided by a normal week on the same shops across 566 NFL home games at these stadiums (+33%) and 19 Copa América 2024 matches (+27%). A citywide visitor lift could not be separated from method noise (Copa 2024 host cities versus Boston, Philadelphia, and Seattle, ±8%). A 2026 match is shown against the same calendar month of 2024. This page is not a forecast.

Visitor share uses CUSTOMER_HOME_CITY: the share of a shop's card customers whose home city is outside the metro's states, 2022-2024, for shops with 20 or more such customers. Readiness scores use Plan D scoring on per-shop rates.
